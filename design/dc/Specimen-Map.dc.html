3 · CELLREADOUT · A READING, NEVER A VERDICT
{{ r.label }}
No duration, no bound, no radius.
A verdict is a claim about a place someone told us they care about. A tapped square has no radius and no spell model, so giving it a sentence would be byge answering a question nobody asked — in the app's own headline voice, on something that does not qualify for it. The card reports what the instrument saw, and offers the one action that turns a reading into an answer.
Anchored to the cell, not a corner: on a running animation, a corner card loses the link to which square you hit within two frames. It flips above when the tap lands low.
4 · TRANSPORT AND LEGEND
Paused at the present, and stopped at the horizon. It does not loop: looping turns a two-hour forecast into wallpaper and erases the frame where the data runs out — the same edge the verdict means by “no end in sight”. At the end it becomes REPLAY, so stopping reads as a state.
Collapsed, it drops the band NAMES and keeps the two non-rain states. The ramp is learnable; “no radar” and “dry” are not guessable, and they are the two the design refuses to leave ambiguous.
