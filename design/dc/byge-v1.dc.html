About byge
{{ markMedium }} byge /ˈbyːɡə/
Norwegian: a passing shower.

byge reads MET Norway’s radar nowcast for your exact coordinate and tells you whether it is raining, and for how long. Nothing else.

Data from MET Norway. Radar nowcast (yrwms-nordic), licensed NLOD 2.0 and CC BY 4.0.
Not affiliated with, or endorsed by, MET Norway or NRK.
Forecast frames are advection-only: the measured rain field slides along measured motion. Cells do not grow or die, so far-out frames are weaker — which is what the confidence marker tells you.
The horizon is 115 minutes. When a spell is still going at the horizon, byge reports a lower bound and says so. It will never round an unknown into a number.
Version 1.0 · offline-capable · settings
The mark, and the install kit
A band crossing a point: the shower passes over where you are. No cloud, no droplet. Two solid shapes, so it survives 16px and arbitrary Android masking.
