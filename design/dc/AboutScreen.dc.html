byge /ˈbyːɡə/
Norwegian: a passing shower.

byge reads MET Norway’s radar for your exact coordinate and tells you whether it is raining, and for how long. Nothing else.

Appearance
Data from MET Norway. The radar nowcast (yrwms-nordic), licensed NLOD 2.0 and CC BY 4.0. byge is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, MET Norway or NRK.
It is radar, not a weather model. byge sees the rain that exists right now and slides it forward along its measured motion. Nothing is invented, and nothing grows or dies — which is why a 20-minute prediction is strong and a 100-minute one is weak. The confidence marker says which you are looking at.
The horizon is 115 minutes. When rain is still falling at the horizon, byge gives you a lower bound and says so. It will never round an unknown into a number.
Where radar cannot see, byge says nothing. Unobserved is not dry. Coverage is the Nordic mosaic — 34 radars across Norway, Sweden and Finland — and its edge is ragged.
It works offline. The last answer for each place is kept, with its age. An old forecast is still a useful forecast, as long as you know it is old.
