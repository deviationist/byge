Band 6 dark was #C77BD6 (0.571) — a 0.144 dip below heavy rain, which put the inversion back at the top of the scale, on the one band where being noticed matters most. Now #E8BCF4 (0.790, +0.074 over band 5), not #E0AEEA: +0.025 is under a noticeable difference at swatch and bar sizes, so it would satisfy the test and fail the eye. The ~288° purple stays as a categorical marker — a different kind of weather — but hue is the second signal, never the only one, because it is the signal a colour-blind reader may not receive.
